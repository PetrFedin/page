02 / METHOD

How the engagement works

No promised improvement percentages upfront. We agree on the measurement method before the work begins.

01Frame the decision

Define the management question and realistic options: buy, reduce, invest, change an operating process or build a system.

02Establish the baseline

Agree on the observation period, grain, formulas and data owners. Source inconsistencies remain visible.

03Compare alternatives

Model several options with costs, constraints and impact on margin and working capital.

04Pilot a bounded workflow

Set an owner, timebox, implementation boundary and observable acceptance criteria.

05Document actual results

Compare forecasts with actuals, retain calculations and decision evidence, and scale only what holds up.

04 / EVIDENCE

What counts as evidence

Method, demonstration and customer adoption are different evidence levels. They should not be conflated.

Methodology

Explains the proposed approach; it is not proof of realized financial improvements.

Working prototype

Shows behavior in tested technical scenarios. It does not prove adoption by an external customer.

Project outcome

Requires metric definitions, time period, source, baseline and permission to publish.
