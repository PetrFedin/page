Privacy policy

Effective from 3 October 2026

Who processes the data

Your data is processed by Petr Fedin, the owner of syntha.pro. You can reach me on Telegram at @sheqel or by phone on +7 977 578 16 85.

What data is collected

  • your name (and, if you choose, surname and patronymic) as entered in the form;
  • the contact details you give: email, Telegram, phone;
  • for an enquiry on behalf of a company: its name, tax ID (INN), address and website;
  • the topic and text of your message;
  • any file attached to the enquiry.

The site also keeps its own visit statistics: the pages and sections opened, clicks, downloads, progress through the form, approximate country and city, device and browser type, IP address and a random visitor identifier stored in the browser. These data are not passed to third-party analytics or advertising services, and visitors are not tracked beyond this site. The statistics show which materials are useful; only the owner has access to them.

Purpose of processing

Personal data is processed to respond to enquiries, manage agreed correspondence and review aggregated usage statistics. It is not used for advertising mailings. Technical service providers may include Netlify (isolated V2 hosting), Cloudflare (the main site and Turnstile protection) and Telegram (delivery to the owner). Only data necessary for those operations is shared through the relevant services.

Storage and transfer of data

When the secure form is configured, an enquiry is sent to the owner’s private Telegram chat after consent and Turnstile verification. The main site may process and store contact records on Cloudflare infrastructure. The isolated Netlify V2 has no separate submissions database; Netlify may maintain technical access logs under its own policies. When private delivery has not been configured, the V2 form remains disabled and direct contacts are available.

Retention

Enquiries are kept for as long as the correspondence needs them; visit statistics for no longer than a year. You can ask me to delete an enquiry at any time: message me on Telegram or contact me on the number above, and the correspondence will be deleted.

Your rights

  • to find out what data of yours I hold;
  • to ask for it to be corrected or deleted;
  • to withdraw your consent, after which processing stops.

Consent

By ticking the consent box in the form, you agree to the processing of the data listed above on the terms of this policy. The form cannot be sent without consent; you can write to me directly on Telegram or WhatsApp instead.
