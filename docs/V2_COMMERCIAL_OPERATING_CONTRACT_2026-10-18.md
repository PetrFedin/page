# V2 — commercial operating contract (pre-production)
**Version:** 18 October 2026. **Status:** implementation-ready in the isolated V2 branch; public commercial operation is NOT admitted.

## Positioning and conversion architecture
The professional identity is the entry point. A visitor should not be required to understand eleven products or buy/adapt/build models before describing a business problem.

Two primary hero actions:
1. **Discuss a business challenge** → actual \`#contact\` with consent and contact method.
2. **Explore digital products** → 3 initial dossier cards and explicit expansion for the remaining 8.

Secondary pathways: CV/experience, partnership/investor, publications, general contact and full professional profile. These remain accessible via a real \`<details>\` element; not silently deleted.

Three segmented journeys:
- **Client:** specific decision problem → clarify data boundary → select one of five services → approved proposal.
- **Implementation partner:** product and technical relationship → bounded pilot → two-sided responsibilities → NDA/terms if needed.
- **Investor:** product stage/ownership constraints → only hypothetical scenarios with assumptions → due diligence request; no equity sale or price claims without sign-off.

## Service offer table — scope and limits

| Service | Evidence needed at discovery | Typical tangible deliverable | Explicit exclusion before scoping |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy and capital | Product-market definition, markets, constraints, management P&L | Options memo with scenarios, assumptions and milestone acceptance | No guarantee of expansion success, valuation or investor financing |
| Commercial model | Channel net sales, discounts, returns, cost-to-serve, assortment | Unit economics by product/channel with reconciled metric definitions | No assumed CAC/LTV or uplift without client records |
| Merchandising and buying | Sales, stock, size, landed cost, purchase orders | Transparent OTB/GMROI/sell-through diagnostics and recommended policy | No automatic buying decisions or fixed improvement percentages |
| Processes and digital systems | Inventory of systems, data contracts, user roles, task workflow | Architecture, data contracts, pilot roadmap, test scenarios and handover | No undocumented ERP integration, 1C access or external vendor binding |
| Analytics and forecasting | Source extracts, dictionaries, grain, plan/fact and business calendar | Defined reporting model, quality rules, relevant forecasts and caveats | No guaranteed forecasting accuracy or unlimited reports before scope |

Engagement format and price are negotiated after discovery. The website must not imply a signed contract, pre-approved budget, fixed delivery date or market discount unsupported by a dated quote.

## Lead workflow
1. \`received\`: transport receipt **verified by provider**; never count a button click as a lead.
2. \`needs_review\`: route, problem, contact, consent and spam-control evidence; user-selected route is NOT a qualified lead.
3. \`contacted\`: an actual response recorded with timestamp and owner.
4. \`scoped\`: discovery outcome and agreed next step; NDA/data scope where relevant.
5. \`proposed\`: a versioned commercial offer with work packages, acceptance, exclusions, timeline, responsibilities and pricing.
6. \`won\` / \`lost\`: based on verified contract or commercial decision, not a calculated investor scenario.

Internal analytics should track only necessary routing metrics: visit → path → dossier/contact opened → provider-acknowledged delivery → human-qualified lead → agreed meeting → proposal → confirmed contract. Respect consent, retention and access restrictions; no unnecessary financial inputs or PII in URLs or public metrics.
