# SYNTHA V2 — commercial offer and investment architecture
**Draft:** 14 October 2026. **Environment:** isolated V2 branch only. V1 / `main` / `syntha.pro` remain unchanged.

## Decision

The public interface should sell **business outcomes with a verifiable decision path**, not solicit funding or promise a launch, a particular investment return, pre-existing unallocated equity, discounted services or a guaranteed delivery date.

The flagship module is a bilingual `#opportunities` section with four switchable routes, an optional product selector spanning the eleven project dossiers, privacy-safe calculators and a direct link to the existing qualified enquiry process. The section is NOT a checkout, brokerage, or funding platform.

|---|---|---|---|

| Procurement / retail / manufacturing management | Planning, ERP/PLM, inventory, supply and BI workflows | Owned data definitions, scenario calculus, role permissions, pilot acceptance | Integration scope, live client performance |
| Startup / product founder | Bespoke software / CRM / marketplace / finance control | Discovery, architecture, build, QA, deployment evidence, agreed IP and maintenance contract | Fixed price until scope and rights confirmed |
| Platform operator | Integration of an existing SYNTHA product module | Working vertical scenario, API/data mapping, test evidence, SLA where contracted | Generalised readiness across every product |
| Design partner | Limited pilot and opportunity to shape product backlog | Named workflow, data consent, acceptance criteria, scope/time boundary | Guaranteed free service or priority rights |
| Founding customer | Potential paid product pre-order or service credit | Specific features, effective date, early benefits, refunds and cancellation terms in signed contract | Launch date, final pricing, unlimited access |
| Investor / strategic partner | Potential primary financing, secondary acquisition, milestone-based funding | Verified title/IP/cap table, valuation evidence, term sheet, tranched budget, monitoring rights | Whether shares or 100% control are actually available |

## Commercial lanes for software

1. **Configure / integrate a platform** — if a working validated module fits the scope; license + implementation + operations must be priced separately.
2. **Build a dedicated system** — independent system ownership, bespoke data model and UX; define what IP transfers and what remains reusable.
3. **Co-build a vertical solution** — sponsor pays for defined functionality while platform owner may retain reusable generic IP; establish exclusivity limits and pricing.
4. **Transform operations before software** — where process changes, governance and reporting deliver value without a new monolithic platform.
5. **Managed product / support** — subscription, enterprise support, integrations and SLA only after production scope is confirmed.

Functional families shown in the calculator: CRM, marketplace, ERP/PLM, finance/treasury control, BI, web/mobile, integration and bespoke systems.

Delivery gate sequence: audit → architecture/scope → working vertical pilot → implementation/security/data migration → actual-results acceptance and support.

### Public calculator definitions

**Bespoke vs reusable code (labour-only):**
- Baseline range = entered hours × $25–49/h Clutch reference range.
- Reuse variant = entered hours × (1 − hypothetical reusable share) × the **same** $25–49/h.
- Week estimate = assumed total hours / entered available team hours/week.
- It is **not** a fixed project quote, a validated reuse coefficient or a comparison against a committed SYNTHA fee.
- Real proposals must additionally model discovery, architecture, migration, QA, privacy/security, hosting, licensing, operations, tax and support.

**Operational redesign:**
- Current annual manual labour cost = entered annual manual hours × fully loaded hourly cost.
- Hypothetical labour saving = baseline labour × entered efficiency share.
- Hypothetical recurring net annual effect = labour saving + independent other savings − incremental annual support costs.
- Simple payback = one-time implementation budget / positive net recurring annual effect; otherwise not calculable.
- No double-counting compensation. Actual savings require operational evidence.

**Early access:**
- Assumed list-equivalent = entered hypothetical future monthly price × discount months.
- Discounted scenario = list equivalent × (1 − hypothetical discount).
- Difference is **not** a guaranteed monetary saving or a public commercial offer.

## Participation structures and ownership integrity

1. **Primary investment:** consideration goes into the issuing company, subject to corporate approvals. Simplified post-money = entered pre-money + new money; investor share = new money/post-money. A pre-existing founder share is diluted pro rata.
2. **Secondary purchase:** price paid to a selling holder; the company receives **no** capital. Approximate transferred percentage = consideration/assumed existing equity value. A founder's post-transaction ownership CANNOT be calculated without knowing who sells.
3. **Milestone-based co-financing:** budget released after verified acceptance — legal structure may be equity, debt, contracted development or license. These are different rights.
4. **Asset or company acquisition:** purchase of selected software IP, enterprise/business or potentially the entire equity structure only **if** holders, contracts and laws permit. **No 100% block is currently advertised as available.**
5. **Reward-style founding customer:** buys defined future services or features, not stock, governance rights, dividends or investor distributions.

The user wishes to retain founder ownership. This is a **negotiating objective**: model an optional pre-round founder percentage and an optional post-round minimum, but do not display an invented present cap table or guaranteed retention floor. Actual beneficial ownership, restrictions, options, licenses and code/IP chain of title must be verified before diligence.

## Investor evidence gates (per project)

Existing `INVESTOR_BLUEPRINTS[projectId][lang].tranches` supplies **three specific milestones per each of the 11 projects**. The Studio displays these beside the project's proposed revenue mechanism and the missing acceptance evidence. This avoids a generic universal fundraising promise.

Proposed use-of-proceeds categories, pending detailed estimate and owner sign-off:
1. Product/security/data rights and operational readiness.
2. Pilot with named business owners, proof of delivery and real unit costs.
3. Distribution, contracts, support and compliance for commercial release.
4. Replicable scale only after paid retention, gross margin and adoption are proven.

No numeric budget allocation percentages or use-of-funds amounts are yet presented as company-approved.

## How to launch free / paid levels without promising impossible benefits

| Stage | Public phrasing | Required operational gate |
|---|---|---|
| Explore | Free public descriptions, access to approved demonstrations | Product demo actually available |
| Design partner | Carefully scoped free or discounted pilot, subject to invitation | Slots, duration, data/IP terms, support and liability approved |
| Founding customer | Optional feature pre-order, early priority or contracted credit | Exact rights, price, delivery/acceptance, refund/cancellation and availability documented |
| Free post-launch | Basic tier **if the product's economics permit** | Unit economics, fair limits, capacity, privacy and operations tested |
| Paid business tier | Integrations, automation, reporting, team workflows and support | Published feature matrix, T&Cs and SLA |
| Enterprise / ownership | Dedicated installation, custom IP/licensing and professional services | Rights and separately scoped quotation |

No payment collection, charge authorization, early-access reservation or transaction execution exists in this draft.

## Privacy and legal review gates

- Financial inputs remain in in-memory UI state only. They are not included in URLs, tracking events, lead payloads, third-party API requests or browser storage.
- Only a selected project ID, generic enquiry route and descriptive request subject can reach the **existing consent-controlled** contact process; backend deployment remains unverified.
- Public equity and crowdfunding advertising, market-specific financial promotion, securities offering exemptions and founder/shareholder consent require qualified jurisdictional legal review. Reward-style pre-sales are not legal substitutes for share issuance.
- Never expose a full cap table, owner identities, customers, secret financials or diligence room contents publicly without rights and consent.
- Investor returns, valuation, launch deadlines, market discounts, audited results and available shares remain **unverified** until specific substantiation exists.
