# A visitor's plan is not a confirmed reservation

**English landing-page article and Telegram copy. Prepared 22 October 2026. HOLD.**

A good city itinerary must distinguish a visit someone hopes to make from a ticket they say they already own. Neither is proof that a venue has confirmed a reservation. Moscow's development branch records these states separately. The same saved trip entry feeds Today and the itinerary; an official venue website link is not presented as a booking transaction.

This integrated candidate is an unmerged Draft PR. GitHub Actions passed for commit bacef5211b317d2a9066b5bb47a278017c83bf87 on 22 October. However, provider inventory, actual bookings, ticket purchases and payments have not been connected. Physical-device admission and third-party media rights are still outstanding.

**Telegram draft:** Planning a museum visit isn't buying a ticket. The Moscow prototype keeps plans and traveller-reported reservations distinct from supplier confirmation. It has passing automated checks, but no live booking connector or public release approval.
